LISTING COPY (plain 'how it works' style, per Peti's preference)

=== LISTING 1: Caliper Wall Mount — Vertical Sheath (mountA_v8.stl) ===
Hangs your 150mm digital caliper on the wall, head up.
Slide the beam into the slot from the top — it drops all the way down and
rests on a stop inside. The caliper carries itself; nothing balances on a
ledge. LCD and scale face out. Lift it out by the head with one hand.
- fits 150mm digital calipers with beam up to 6.2 x 16mm (incl. scale rail)
- prints flat, no supports, one screw
- 95mm tall, mounts with a single countersunk screw
HOW IT HOLDS: blade bottom rests on a stop inside the slot — the caliper's
own weight keeps it seated. Bump-safe: it can't lever out.

=== LISTING 2: Caliper Desk Trough (Kövér v3, 84mm) ===
Lay your caliper flat on the desk or shelf, LCD up.
The slider end sits in its own wide bay, the blade runs along a fitted
channel — no wobble, no rolling off. Drain holes in the channel floor.
- fits 150mm digital calipers, jaw span to 76.5mm
- prints flat, no supports

=== CABLE CLIPS (existing v2 snap-fit, 3 sizes) ===
Press the cable in from the top — it snaps past the throat and sits in the
channel. Pull to release. No threading, screw or zip tie needed.
- 6.4 / 8.4 / 10.4mm channel (cable + 0.4mm tolerance)
- snap throat grips the cable; adhesive flat + screw hole on the back
